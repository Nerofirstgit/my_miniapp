The Chinese hero wordmark uses a subset of Noto Sans SC, weight 900,
covering “你好，我叫康彤” and the original text “天工开物工作室”.

Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanssc

The local WOFF2 and Three.js JSON outlines derive from the Google Fonts text
subset endpoint. See `NotoSansSC-OFL.txt` for the copyright notice and license.
